Her Story

Virginia Cathey, founder of Virginia Cathey Collections, is a Brooklyn-born fashion designer, entrepreneur, and creative visionary with over 40 years of sewing, tailoring, and fashion expertise. Known for her signature hooded bodysuits, reversible garments, and wearable couture designs, Virginia has been featured in more than 60 publications, including Harper's Bazaar Vietnam and Style & Soul Magazine, and has showcased her collections during New York Fashion Week.


As her own seamstress, tailor, stylist, and promoter, she creates versatile designs ranging from ready-to-wear and business attire to red carpet, bridal, eveningwear, and custom couture for women of all sizes and backgrounds. Her work blends elegance, confidence, functionality, and individuality, making luxury fashion feel both empowering and accessible.


Beyond fashion, Virginia brings over 20 years of executive-level operations and event management experience, more than 10 years developing sought-after shea butter wellness products, and years in financial education. Her mission remains the same: to inspire confidence, provide value, and leave a lasting impression through excellence and authenticity.

The Designer

Born to Create

Fashion has been in her family for generations. Born and raised in Brooklyn, NY, Virginia grew up surrounded by creators — her mother, her grandmother, an artistic family touching every art imaginable. When people ask how long she's been designing, her answer is immediate: "Straight out the womb."


She created her entire wedding wardrobe herself. That same devotion — to the most important moments in a woman's life — has driven her work ever since. From hosting and planning multiple beauty pageants and fashion shows featuring original designs, to creating her own original fabric prints, Virginia's creative vision is singular and relentless.


Her core values are immovable: supportive energy, and excellence as the baseline — she always goes above it.

The Origin

Twenty years ago, Virginia wasn't trying to start a business.

She was trying to save her hair. Years of chemical straighteners and perms had taken their toll, causing significant hair loss. Rather than accept the damage, she turned inward — and to nature.


She began blending organic butters and oils by hand, experimenting until the formula felt right. People noticed the smell first. They always do — rich, warm, natural, unmistakable. Then came the questions: what are you using? What is that in your hair?


And then came the moment she never expected. The years of damage — the breakage, the thinning, the chemical scarring — slowly, quietly reversing. The blend was working.


What started in her kitchen, passed between family and friends, has quietly grown into something people swear by. Not just for hair. For skin. For body. For everything it touches. Small operation. Intentional growth. The same love in every jar.
