The Designer

Born to Create

Fashion has been in her family for generations. Born and raised in Brooklyn, NY, Virginia grew up surrounded by creators — her mother, her grandmother, an artistic family touching every art imaginable. When people ask how long she's been designing, her answer is immediate: "Straight out the womb."


She created her entire wedding wardrobe herself. That same devotion to the most important moments in a woman's life has driven her work ever since. From hosting and planning multiple beauty pageants and fashion shows featuring original designs, to creating her own original fabric prints — Virginia's creative vision is singular and relentless.


Her core values are immovable: supportive energy, and excellence as the baseline. She always goes above it.
